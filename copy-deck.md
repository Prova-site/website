## 2. Intro: the problem

**H2:** AI scales. Responsibility doesn't.

AI already runs at the scale of entire societies. The responsibility that should come with it still lives in pilot projects, side teams and position papers.

Policy rarely sets a vision for where we want to go. When it does, it seldom reaches the products people use. And the products are still designed around foundation models, not around the people who live and work with them.

Prova exists to close that gap, one use case at a time.

## 5. The name

**H2:** Why we're called Prova

In Italian, *prova* means a test, a proof and a rehearsal. Today AI is shipped first and questioned later. We think AI should be rehearsed and iterated right from the start, with real data, real rules and the people it will affect, while changing course is still cheap. 

Caption: How AI reaches us today → **Ship**
Caption: How it should reach us instead → **Prova**
